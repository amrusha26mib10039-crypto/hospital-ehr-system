# Hospital Patient & Electronic Health Record (EHR) System

## 1. Problem Statement

Hospitals require a systematic approach to handle information regarding patients, prescriptions, and billing-related information. Handling the above-mentioned information manually leads to difficulties in maintaining and accessing the information.

The Hospital Patient & Electronic Health Record (EHR) System is designed as a Python-based application that helps to manage hospital operations and patient information systematically.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of this project consists of managing basic hospital and patient activities using a Python-based application.

It  includes the following points:

- Management of patient registration and patient information
- Information regarding Doctors
- Patient diagnosis and patient allergy information
- Chemist and medicine information
- Medicine billing
- Hospital record management

## 3. Target Users

The target users of the above mentioned application are as follows:

- Hospital Personnel:
- Hospital Receptionists
- Doctors
- Chemists

## 4. High-Level Features

Major features of the application consist of:

- Patient registration
- Patient information management
- Doctor information and diagnosis
- Recording patient allergy information
- Chemist and medicine information management
- Medicine billing
- Menu-driven interface
- Hospital record management
